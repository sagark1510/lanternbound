Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

The short version: Lanternbound does not collect any personal information. It never stores your data on our servers, shares it or sells it. The game runs entirely on your device and works offline.

This Privacy Policy explains how TRENTIUM SOLUTION PRIVATE LIMITED ("we", "us") handles information in the Lanternbound mobile game ("the App") for iOS, iPadOS and Android.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not:

  • require an account, sign-in, name, email address or phone number;
  • send any data to us or to any server;
  • include analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs;
  • access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone;
  • track you across other apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

To save your progress, the App stores game data locally on your device. This includes your current run, your statistics and your Options settings (sound effects, music and haptics). This data never leaves your device and we cannot access it. Deleting the App removes it.

Third-party services

The App uses no third-party services that collect data. Apple and Google may collect information when you download apps from their stores. That is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases and advertising

The App contains no in-app purchases and no advertising.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children of any age.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You can remove all locally stored game data at any time by deleting the App.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the App handles information, we will update this page and its effective date. Material changes will also be reflected in an App update.
