FAQ

How do I play a card?

Drag a card upward to play it. Attack and debuff cards are dragged onto an enemy; if only one enemy is left, they target it automatically. Tap a card to inspect it and read what its keywords mean.

What do the icons above enemies mean?

They show what each enemy will do on its next turn: a sword means it will attack, a shield means it will block, arrows mean a buff or debuff, and a flame means Burn. Tap an enemy to see its intent and statuses in detail.

What is Light?

Light is your lantern's charge, up to 10. Some cards gather it, and others such as Blazing Edge, Mirror Flame, Eclipse and Starfall grow stronger with it or spend it for big effects.

What are the different rooms on the map?

Fights earn new cards. Elites are tougher and grant +6 Max HP. At a Campfire you can heal 30% or purge a card from your deck. Shrines offer a rare card or +8 Max HP. The Drowned Bell waits at the bottom.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your run is saved automatically after every room, so you can close the app and choose Continue later. Saves stay on your device. The game has no accounts or cloud storage, so deleting the app also deletes its progress.

How do I turn off music, sound or vibration?

Open Options from the main menu (or during a run) and switch Sound Effects, Music or Haptics on or off. Your choices are saved on your device.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Lanternbound works fully offline. It has no ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases.

The game feels slow. What can I try?

Close other apps and restart the game, and check that your device isn't in a battery-saving mode. Updating your device's OS can also help. If it keeps happening, email us.
